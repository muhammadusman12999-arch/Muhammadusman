About me

I make digital products
easier to use.

What I doUX + UI

I start with what people need to do.
Then make it easier.

Messy workflowClear UX process

I like untangling busy workflows and making the next step obvious. I map the flow, design the screens, prototype key interactions and give developers the details they need to build it.

My UI/UX design processSix steps
  1. 01
    Research

    Talk to users, study competitors and find the real problem.

  2. 02
    Define

    Turn insights into personas, goals and clear requirements.

  3. 03
    Wireframe

    Map user flows and sketch low-fidelity layouts.

  4. 04
    UI Design

    Craft high-fidelity screens, styles and a design system.

  5. 05
    Prototype & Test

    Build clickable prototypes and test them with real users.

  6. 06
    Handoff

    Share specs, assets and components with developers.
